About Truth Label

Truth Label is an open-source initiative dedicated to honesty in the aisles. We use barcode scanning technology to verify sustainability claims, ensuring that the "eco-friendly" or "healthy" products you choose actually live up to their promises. No more guessing, just the truth

Our mission is to empower consumers with accurate information, helping them make informed choices that align with their diets. By exposing health washing and greenwashing tactics, we aim to create a marketplace where transparency is the norm and trust is earned.

What is Open Food Facts?

Open Food Facts is a free, open, and collaborative database of food products from around the world, created by thousands of volunteers. It includes detailed information about each product such as ingredients, allergens, nutritional values (including Nutri-Score), packaging, brands, labels (organic, gluten-free, vegan, etc.), and traceability data. The database is open data, licensed for reuse by anyone for any purpose, and it supports apps for scanning products to access and contribute data. It aims to help consumers make better food choices and also supports scientific and educational uses. The project was launched in 2012 and now contains millions of products contributed by a global community through apps and websites
